RoPOS is a point of sale system for Roblox: staff ring up orders at a till, the customer approves the payment on their own screen, and the items land in their backpack. Prices are in pounds.

## Which file do I download?

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| **RoPOS-Studio.zip** | Everything for Roblox Studio. Unzip it and open `READ ME FIRST.txt` |
| **RoPOS.rbxlx** | Just the demo place. Open it in Roblox Studio and press Play |
| **RoPOS-Simulator.html** | Try it in a browser, no Roblox needed. Double-click to open |

## Quick start in Roblox Studio

1. Open `RoPOS.rbxlx` and press **Play**.
2. Walk behind the Blox Mart counter and hold **E** on the till.
3. Open a shift on the **Shift** tab, add items on **Sell**, choose yourself as the customer and press **Charge**.
4. Press **Pay** on your payment screen.

To add RoPOS to your own game, follow `READ ME FIRST.txt` in the zip.
