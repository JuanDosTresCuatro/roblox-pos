    Things to try
    1. On the black kiosk, press Touch to start, pick Eat in, add items and Pay. Watch order 1 appear on the board and the kitchen display.
    2. Press Ready on the kitchen display: the order moves to Ready and Bob is told.
    3. Add items, choose Bob and press Charge. Pay on Bob's screen.
    4. Charge Eve while she is away from the counter, then switch her At the counter on.
    5. Charge a Golden Crown: Bob can't afford £150.
    6. Switch to Cass: discounts stop at 10% and the manager tabs disappear.
    7. As Ann, change a price while a payment is waiting, then pay.
    8. Refund a sale on the Sales tab, then try to refund it again.
    9. Close the shift for the Z report.
